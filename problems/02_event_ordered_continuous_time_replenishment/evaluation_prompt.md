We have a replenishment routing problem for materials such as industrial gas, fuel, or liquid raw material that customers continuously consume. Each customer has an on-site tank: too little inventory disrupts production, while too much inventory exceeds tank capacity. At the end of the planning horizon each customer must also retain a required reserve. The warehouse has enough supply, and vehicles may leave the warehouse and, if necessary, return to reload. We need to decide when vehicles depart, which customers they visit in what order, and how much to deliver at each visit. The data provide consumption rates, initial inventories, lower and upper tank limits, terminal requirements, vehicle capacity, travel times, and travel costs. Some customers may need more than one delivery in the day. The goal is to avoid stockouts and overflow during operations, satisfy terminal inventory requirements, and minimize total travel cost.
