\begin{abstract}
Retail assortment planning converts a calibrated substitution model into a discrete display decision. Under the paired combinatorial logit model, every unordered product pair remains a choice nest when one or both products are offered. This candidate isolates that semantic requirement in a six-product, cardinality-constrained instance with a structured exact MILP and a complete enumeration oracle. The unique optimal assortment is $\{3,5,6\}$. Two independently generated programs implement different incorrect pair-state systems and both choose $\{2,3,5,6\}$, whose correct expected margin is 11.64\% below optimum. The programs solve successfully; the error lies entirely in translating the calibrated demand model.
\end{abstract}

\section{Business Brief}

\begin{businessbrief}
Our category team must choose up to four products for a limited retail display. For each product, the demand-science team has estimated a customer-attraction weight and a normalized contribution margin. It has also calibrated a standard paired combinatorial logit (PCL) choice model, with a dissimilarity estimate for every unordered product pair and an outside-option weight for customers who leave without purchasing. Choose the assortment that maximizes expected contribution margin while accounting for substitution among the offered products.
\end{businessbrief}

Naming the calibrated PCL engine makes the business request operationally precise while leaving the probability system and optimization formulation to the modeler.

\section{PCL Choice System}

Let $N$ be the products, $x_i\in\{0,1\}$ indicate whether product $i$ is offered, $v_i>0$ be attraction, $r_i\ge0$ be normalized margin, $v_0>0$ be outside-option weight, and $\gamma_{ij}\in(0,1]$ be pair dissimilarity. For pair $\{i,j\}$, define
\begin{align}
I_{ij}&=\left(v_i^{1/\gamma_{ij}}+v_j^{1/\gamma_{ij}}\right)^{\gamma_{ij}}, \label{eq:nest}\\
\mu_{ij}^i&=I_{ij}\frac{v_i^{1/\gamma_{ij}}}{v_i^{1/\gamma_{ij}}+v_j^{1/\gamma_{ij}}},\qquad
\mu_{ij}^j=I_{ij}-\mu_{ij}^i. \label{eq:mass}
\end{align}
Use state binaries $y_{ij}^{10}$, $y_{ij}^{01}$, and $y_{ij}^{11}$ for only $i$, only $j$, and both products offered. They satisfy
\begin{align}
y_{ij}^{11}&\le x_i,\quad y_{ij}^{11}\le x_j,\quad
y_{ij}^{11}\ge x_i+x_j-1, \label{eq:and}\\
y_{ij}^{10}&=x_i-y_{ij}^{11},\qquad
y_{ij}^{01}=x_j-y_{ij}^{11}. \label{eq:states}
\end{align}
The standard PCL denominator and expected-margin numerator are
\begin{align}
D(x)&=v_0+\sum_{i<j}\left(v_i y_{ij}^{10}+v_j y_{ij}^{01}+I_{ij}y_{ij}^{11}\right), \label{eq:denom}\\
R(x)&=\sum_{i<j}\left(r_iv_i y_{ij}^{10}+r_jv_j y_{ij}^{01}
+(r_i\mu_{ij}^i+r_j\mu_{ij}^j)y_{ij}^{11}\right). \label{eq:numer}
\end{align}
The exactly-one-offered states in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:denom}--\eqref{eq:numer} are decisive: a pair vanishes only when neither member is offered.

\section{Structured Exact MILP}

The assortment problem maximizes $R(x)/D(x)$ subject to $\sum_i x_i\le4$. Apply a Charnes--Cooper transformation with $t=1/D(x)$ and $w_{ij}^s=t y_{ij}^s$ for $s\in\{10,01,11\}$. Let $b_{ij}^s$ and $a_{ij}^s$ denote the denominator and numerator coefficients in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:denom} and~\eqref{eq:numer}. Valid bounds $0<\underline t\le t\le\overline t$ follow from $v_0\le D(x)\le\overline D$. The exact MILP is
\begin{align}
\max\quad &\sum_{i<j}\sum_s a_{ij}^s w_{ij}^s, \label{eq:milpobj}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad
&v_0t+\sum_{i<j}\sum_s b_{ij}^s w_{ij}^s=1, \label{eq:normalize}\\
&\sum_{i\in N}x_i\le4, \quad \text{Eqs.~\eqref{eq:and}--\eqref{eq:states}}, \label{eq:cardinality}\\
&\underline t y_{ij}^s\le w_{ij}^s\le\overline t y_{ij}^s
&&\forall i<j,s, \label{eq:scaleone}\\
&t-\overline t(1-y_{ij}^s)\le w_{ij}^s
\le t-\underline t(1-y_{ij}^s)
&&\forall i<j,s, \label{eq:scaletwo}\\
&x_i,y_{ij}^s\in\{0,1\},\quad t,w_{ij}^s\ge0. \label{eq:domain}
\end{align}
Constraints~\eqref{eq:scaleone}--\eqref{eq:scaletwo} impose $w_{ij}^s=t y_{ij}^s$. The repository includes this structured Gurobi model and a separate exhaustive oracle over all 57 assortments of size at most four.

\section{Exact Instance and Failure Certificate}

The six products are derived from the official MIT-licensed correlated synthetic instance of Feng, Che, and Chen~\cite{feng2026}. Product IDs are remapped, and $v_0=3.84538427435401$ preserves a no-purchase probability of 0.25 when all six products are displayed.

\begin{center}
\captionof{table}{Exact optimum and independently generated decisions.}
\begin{tabularx}{0.94\linewidth}{lY r}
\toprule
Model & Selected products & Objective under that model \\
\midrule
Correct standard PCL & $\{3,5,6\}$ & 0.323647238294198 \\
Generated singleton-plus-pair model & $\{2,3,5,6\}$ & 0.245658160940 \\
Generated jointly-offered-pairs-only model & $\{2,3,5,6\}$ & 0.188961826760 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The first generated program adds one global singleton term per selected product, instead of one exactly-one-offered contribution for every absent counterpart. The second retains a pair nest only when both products are offered. Both programs are executable and prove their own MILP optima.

\evidencebox{The correct PCL oracle has the unique optimum $\{3,5,6\}$ with expected margin 0.323647238294198. Both generated programs choose $\{2,3,5,6\}$. Evaluated under the calibrated standard PCL model, that decision is worth 0.285975423739680, giving absolute regret 0.037671814554518 and relative regret 11.639776\%.}
